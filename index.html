This guide breaks down the top destinations in Southern Oregon by what matters most to buyers: affordability, schools, amenities, parks, and community.

The Top Destinations in Southern Oregon

Evaluated by what matters most to buyers: real estate value, school quality, parks, everyday amenities, and unique community culture.

Commercial Center of the Rogue Valley

Medford, Oregon

Convenience, Connectivity & Strong Job Market

Medford is a practical choice and the commercial center of the Rogue Valley. It offers convenience, connectivity, and a strong job market, making it ideal for young professionals and families seeking a suburban lifestyle.

Real Estate

With a median home price of around $427,000, your dollar stretches far. Established, upscale neighborhoods dominate East Medford (such as the Cherry Lane area), with newer, more affordable developments in North Medford.

Schools & Parks

The Medford School District is notable for its excellent Magnet programs and higher education pathways. Outdoor enthusiasts can enjoy the over 30 parks with standouts including the 110-acre Bear Creek Park and the expansive 1,740-acre Prescott Park.

Amenities & Lifestyle

Healthcare is a major economic driver, followed by retail, and automotive retail. The Rogue Valley Mall and the regional airport add everyday convenience. Most essentials are within a five-minute drive.

Culture

Medford hosts events year-round from April’s Pear Blossom Festival to multiple comic cons. RogueX and Pear Blossom Park keep kids entertained with plenty of fun for everyone throughout the year.

The Trade-Off

As the commercial center, it experiences more traffic, suburban sprawl, and warmer summer temperatures on the valley floor, with less of the quaint, walkable village charm found in smaller neighboring towns.

Art Scene & Natural Splendor

Ashland, Oregon

Thriving Arts, Lithia Park & Walkable Charm

Ashland’s most notable features are the thriving art scene and stunning natural beauty of Lithia Park. The strongly independent town is highly walkable and consistently ranks among the best small towns in America.

Real Estate

Median home prices are around $515,000. Popular areas include the trendy, walkable Railroad District and the prestigious historic homes surrounding Lithia Park.

Schools & Parks

Education is a major strength, featuring a ~94% graduation rate and a top-10 regional ranking. The 93-acre Lithia Park stretches directly from the downtown plaza up into the mountains.

Amenities & Lifestyle

The world-famous Oregon Shakespeare Festival operates almost year-round as Ashland’s largest employer and cultural centerpiece. Combined with Southern Oregon University, the town maintains a vibrant, progressive energy. The Mt. Ashland Ski Area has winter sports, summer hiking areas, and community events.

Culture

Oregon Shakespeare Festival and SOU’s shows and events are the highlight of Ashland’s cultural scene. City-wide events, like the exciting Festival of Lights in December and acclaimed Ashland Independent Film Festival, bring the city together.

The Trade-Off

Cost of living and property taxes are higher here than in neighboring towns.

Rogue River Adventure & Acreage

Grants Pass, Oregon

Classic Small-Town Americana Along the River

Situated along the world-famous Rogue River, Grants Pass delivers a classic, small-town Americana feel perfect for outdoor enthusiasts and buyers looking for more acreage.

Real Estate

Homes in Grants Pass are more affordable, with average home prices around $378,000. Options range from historic downtown bungalows to premium riverfront properties.

Schools & Parks

The community-focused Rogue River School District features a new early learning and wellness center. Riverside Park offers excellent river access and weekend picnic grounds.

Amenities & Lifestyle

Revitalized Historic G Street serves as the community core, packed with antique stores, local breweries, dining, and outdoor gear outfits.

The Trade-Off

It is sleepier, has less nightlife, and it’s further from major retail hubs and the Medford airport.

Historic Gold Rush Town

Jacksonville, Oregon

Preserved 1850s Elegance, Vineyards & Britt Festival

As a perfectly preserved 1850s gold rush town surrounded by rolling vineyards, Jacksonville presents as the Rogue Valley’s top charming and historic town.

The Real Estate

A premier location with a high median home price around $693,000. Properties in the historic core are subject to strict architectural preservation guidelines to maintain the town's character.

Schools & Parks

Older students generally attend the Medford School District or local private institutions. Jacksonville has several parks with the most impressive being the gorgeous 18+ miles of hiking trails in the Jacksonville Woodlands trail system.

Amenities & Lifestyle

Surrounded by world-class Rogue Valley vineyards and tasting rooms. During the summer, the outdoor Britt Music & Arts Festival boosts the local economy.

The Trade-Off

Summer tourist traffic for wine tasting and the Britt Festival can congest the small streets.

Median Home Price: ~$693,000

Central Point, Oregon

Space, Modern Growth & Celebrated Artisan Heritage

Located just minutes north of Medford, Central Point is a sweet spot for buyers looking for more space. It blends a tight-knit community rooted in agricultural heritage with modern residential growth. In 2026, Central Point ranks among the top-10 safest cities in Oregon.

The Real Estate

Median home prices average $407,000. The city is experiencing a surge in new construction, particularly in the popular Twin Creeks development and a revitalized downtown.

Schools & Parks

Central Point School District 6 offers 6 elementary schools, 2 middle schools, and 1 high school. Neighborhood highlights include Twin Creeks Park and Don Jones Memorial Park, featuring splash pads, sports courts, and modern playgrounds.

Amenities & Lifestyle

Famous for its "Artisan Corridor," home to Buttercloud Bakery, Tiffany’s Marketplace, and the world-renowned Rogue Creamery (winner of World's Best Cheese). The nearby Jackson County Expo hosts local fairs, concerts, and events year-round.

The Trade-Off

It lacks the variety of Medford or the historic pedigree of Jacksonville, delivering a more modest experience.

Median Home Price: ~$407,000
